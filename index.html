01 / BANNER
Banner Collection
Graphic Design

A set of promotional banners built for a run of seasonal campaigns, each one designed around a single clear message so it reads in the first half-second of a scroll. I leaned on bold color blocking and a tight grid to keep every version consistent, then adjusted the palette and imagery per campaign so the set never felt repetitive.

02 / POSTER
Poster Collection
Poster Design

A series of event and matchday posters, starting with a commemorative piece for a historic derby rivalry — bold type and layered color built to carry the tension of the occasion. The rest of the set follows that same instinct: strong typographic hierarchy, restrained color, and enough visual weight to hold up printed large.

1 / 5
03 / LOGO
Logo & Mark Design
Visual Identity

A small collection of logo and mark explorations for brands that needed something they could carry across a business card and a billboard without losing clarity. Each mark was built simple enough to work at favicon size, distinct enough to hold its own next to competitors already crowding the same shelf.

04 / FILM
Commercial Film I
AI Video

A short commercial built almost entirely with AI video tools, made to test how far an AI pipeline could carry a real product story. The brief called for something that felt shot, not generated — so most of the effort went into lighting continuity and pacing between shots rather than the individual frames themselves, and that's the part that ended up carrying it.

05 / FILM
Commercial Film II
AI Video

The same campaign reworked as a vertical cut for Stories and Reels. Rather than just cropping the landscape version, I re-cut the pacing entirely — shorter beats, a stronger opening frame, and captions baked into the composition so it still lands with the sound off.

06 / MOTION
From Static to Motion
Motion Design

A static key visual from an earlier campaign, brought back and rebuilt as motion. The layout didn't need to change — it just needed to breathe: subtle parallax, light drifting across the frame, small details animating in. A good composition should survive that kind of push, and this one did.

WHAT I DO.

From the first visual idea to the final frame, I focus on creating work that feels intentional, recognizable and visually refined.

01
Graphic Design

Composition, typography and hierarchy first, always — I want a piece to read in under a second before it's asked to do anything more. That covers logos and visual identity systems as much as it does campaign graphics, banners, posters and everyday digital content.

02
AI Art Direction

I treat AI image and video tools the way I'd treat a photo shoot: a clear reference, consistent lighting and color across every frame, and enough back-and-forth with the tool that the original idea survives the process instead of getting flattened by it.

03
Motion Design

Most of my motion work starts from a still that already has good bones — the job is deciding what moves first, how long it holds, and where it lands. Rhythm and timing usually do more of the storytelling than any single effect.

04
AI Video

End-to-end concept-to-cut production for ads and social content, built around AI video tools without letting it look that way. The brief is almost always the same: make it feel shot, not generated.

6+ YEARS OF CREATIVE EXPERIENCE

I'm Farshad Fatemifar, a graphic designer and AI creator with more than six years of experience working across visual communication, digital design and creative production.

My professional journey began with freelance graphic design in 2020, followed by two years of company experience between 2023 and 2025. Since 2025, I've returned to freelance work with a stronger focus on AI-powered image, video and motion production.

My approach is rooted in graphic design first: composition, typography, hierarchy, storytelling and visual identity. AI is an extension of that foundation — a tool for exploring stronger ideas, faster iterations and new visual possibilities.

I care about making technology serve the idea, not the other way around.
