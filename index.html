6+ YEARS OF CREATIVE EXPERIENCE

I'm Farshad Fatemifar, a graphic designer and AI creator with more than six years of experience working across visual communication, digital design and creative production.

My professional journey began with freelance graphic design in 2020, followed by two years of company experience between 2023 and 2025. Since 2025, I've returned to freelance work with a stronger focus on AI-powered image, video and motion production.

My approach is rooted in graphic design first: composition, typography, hierarchy, storytelling and visual identity. AI is an extension of that foundation — a tool for exploring stronger ideas, faster iterations and new visual possibilities.

I care about making technology serve the idea, not the other way around.
